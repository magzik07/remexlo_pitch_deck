# Remexlo Pitch Deck

A professional pitch deck for **Remexlo** - a marketplace platform connecting skilled craftsmen with customers.

## About Remexlo

Remexlo is a digital marketplace that bridges the gap between skilled craftsmen (carpenters, plumbers, electricians, painters, etc.) and customers who need their services. The platform offers:

- Verified craftsmen profiles with portfolio showcases
- Transparent pricing and instant quotes
- Rating and review system for quality assurance
- Secure payment processing with escrow
- Mobile-first approach for easy access

## Pitch Deck Contents

The pitch deck (`Remexlo_Pitch_Deck.pptx`) includes the following slides:

1. **Title Slide** - Introduction to Remexlo
2. **Problem** - Market challenges for craftsmen and customers
3. **Solution** - How Remexlo addresses these challenges
4. **Market Opportunity** - Market size and growth potential
5. **Business Model** - Revenue streams and monetization
6. **Traction & Roadmap** - Current metrics and future plans
7. **Team** - Leadership and expertise
8. **The Ask** - Investment requirements and use of funds
9. **Contact** - How to reach us
